# Series Bible

**Series:** Teddy & Ichigo

## Art style (LOCKED - never change this)

Painterly storybook illustration. Soft gouache and watercolour texture with
visible brushwork, gentle paper grain, and soft edges where fur meets air.
Hand-painted children's picture book feel. Never glossy 3D, never flat vector,
never photographic.

Warm cosy lighting with one clear source, usually lamplight, candlelight or late
afternoon sun through a window. Light pools on the characters and falls off into
soft shadow.

Palette: warm apricot, honey gold, soft cream, dusty sage green, muted teal, and
warm greys. Slightly desaturated and harmonious. Nothing neon, nothing harsh.

EYES, both characters, never change this: large, round, SOLID glossy black bead
eyes, like real hamster eyes. A single small white catchlight highlight each, and
nothing more. No visible white sclera. No visible iris or pupil separation. No
human-shaped eyes, no eyelids, no eyebrows, no eyelashes. The eyes should read as
dark, wet, shining beads. This is what keeps them cute rather than uncanny.

## World

A cosy attic flat scaled for hamsters. Recurring rooms: a small kitchen with
mismatched crockery and jars of seeds, a living room with a sagging patchwork
sofa and too many houseplants, a bedroom with a nest of blankets, and a round
window that looks out over rooftops.

## Teddy

- **Breed:** Long-haired Syrian, a teddy bear hamster. Male.
- **Appearance:** Large, round and plush, roughly twice Ichigo's size. Long
  shaggy apricot-orange fur that sticks out in soft tufts and never quite lies
  flat, especially around his cheeks and rump. Pale cream belly, chest and
  muzzle. Small rounded dark ears mostly buried in fur. Large dark glossy eyes
  set wide, solid glossy black with a single catchlight. Short blunt muzzle.
  Stubby little paws that look too small for him.
- **Personality:** The rational one. Thinks things through, states the obvious
  truth nobody wanted said, and is usually right in a way that is funnier than
  being wrong. Dry, deadpan wit. Patient with Ichigo in a way that reads as
  quiet devotion rather than tolerance. Big and calm, rarely rattled.

## Ichigo

- **Breed:** Winter white dwarf hamster. Female.
- **Appearance:** Small, compact and neat, roughly half Teddy's size. Short
  smooth dense fur in pale silvery white, with a soft charcoal-grey dorsal
  stripe running from between her ears down her spine, and faint grey shading on
  her flanks. Pure white belly and feet. Proportionally larger ears than Teddy,
  rounded and grey-tipped. Round solid glossy black bead eyes with a single
  catchlight, bright and alert. Fine pointed muzzle with prominent whiskers.
- **Personality:** Sassy, cheeky and very expressive. Acts first and reasons
  afterwards. Teases Teddy constantly, mostly because she likes the reaction.
  Enormous confidence for something that small. Loves him obviously and would
  rather be caught doing anything else than admitting it.

## The comedy engine

Ichigo does something impulsive, sassy or ill-advised. Teddy responds with calm
logic, which either defuses it or makes it much funnier. Sometimes he is
over-thinking something simple and she punctures it in one move.

He is the straight man, she is the chaos. Their size difference is a running
visual joke by itself: something enormous and gentle being bossed around by
something tiny and certain.

## Never do

- No text, letters, numbers, signs, logos or speech bubbles anywhere in the image.
- Never change the art style, fur colours, markings or size difference between
  episodes. Teddy is always visibly much bigger than Ichigo.
- No human characters, no human hands, no modern technology like phones or
  laptops.
- Nothing mean-spirited, scary or sad-ending. They always end up fine.
- Never make them glossy, plastic, hyper-real or anime styled.
- Never give them human eyes. No white sclera, no visible iris rings, no
  eyebrows, no eyelashes. Solid dark bead eyes only.
- Both characters must always be painted in exactly the same style, by the same
  hand, with the same brushwork and the same palette.
- No clothing, scarves, hats, clips or accessories of any kind. Bare fur only.
